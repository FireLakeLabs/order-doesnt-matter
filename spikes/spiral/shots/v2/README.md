# Finding now — screenshot evidence

`*-before.jpg` / `*-after.jpg` are matched fly-through pairs: the same queried
program, turn 3 at phase 0, the same 1440 × 1000 browser viewport, reduced to
1280 pixels wide. The old study's camera framing is preserved in “before”; the
new framing leaves room for the gate. Five programs: full-piece, alternation,
every-four, polymeter, density-64 (512 onsets per cycle).

Four real screenshot iterations (all five programs each):

1. `*-pass1.jpg`: strong falloff, four future turns, radial sound bands, duration
   lighting, gate/rim, central readout. Judged clearer, but readout hid drums and
   future labels collided.
2. `*-pass2.jpg`: names moved beside the gate; central card moved aside; future
   labels separated; calmer future restored slightly. Captured while paused
   around turn 3.32. Dense active notes were still too small.
3. `*-pass3.jpg`: luminous active beads, quieter secondary readout. Same phase
   range. All five frames were inspected together, then dense was inspected at
   full resolution. Active beads are retained.
4. `*-pass4.jpg`: label placement checked around turn 3.67; names sit outside
   the rim on the left half, keeping active spans uncovered. Final “after”
   frames use this placement at exactly turn 3.00. Contact sheets retain each pass.

`signals-after.png`: continuous lane beads in fly-through.
`ring-after.jpg` / `coil-after.jpg`: shared present cues in the other views.
`phone-after.jpg`: 390 × 844 responsive viewport, no horizontal overflow.
`six-voice-pair.jpg`: compact before/after contact sheet.

These are design judgments, not measured viewer recognition times. No stranger
was tested. Notes light for scored spans; release, sample length, delay, and
reverb are not inferred. Sound colours identify Strudel sound names; branches
sharing a sound also share a radial band and focus target.
