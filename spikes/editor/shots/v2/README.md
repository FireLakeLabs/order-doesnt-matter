1. **Round 1 vs baseline.** Names and song structure replaced anonymous path rows. Real House and Techno immediately became easier to navigate. The long songs were no longer duplicate arrangement branches. However, their setting cells incorrectly included unrelated shared definitions, inherited from the old target traversal. The local trance host was still broken. Constellation headings and settings still competed at high counts.
2. **Round 2 vs round 1.** Setting queries were restricted to each instrument. The long-song sheet became meaningful instead of a global list repeated in every row. The arrangement and inactive state worked, but repeated “no events” captions were noisy, chords were superimposed, and the outer settings still clipped. Trance showed that short fixtures had not exercised long custom-helper chains.
3. **Round 3 vs round 2.** Separate pitch tracks exposed chords and stacked samples. Signal curves came from 64 real queries, not assumed automation grids. Inactive constants retained their values. The 20-voice view improved, but Fit still used the former canvas width, long rewrite stacks exceeded cards, and shared editing needed an explicit scope notice. The missing draw parameter still prevented the local trance query.
4. **Round 4 vs round 3.** Shell spacing is bounded; moon placement searches for collision-free positions while preserving shell ownership. Titles, shell labels and driver links have reserved space. The local trance piece now evaluates. Shared and section-specific edits say where they apply. Final refinements retained author names in override search paths, separated room membership by instrument, made Play/jump honor the viewed section, and fixed old/new shared bindings at cycle boundaries. Final screenshots were recaptured after those fixes.

Still poor: Constellation uses substantial vertical space; 12–20 voices require scrolling or search. Dense composite settings aggregate several part values; opening the group reveals their separate edits. Dense pitch lanes cap visible tracks at eight and parts at 400; labels/tooltips retain queried values. Samples, tails, rooms and acoustic continuity are not established by these images. No first-time human usability test was performed.
