Shows

The score becomes a pinball route. Bass notes hold two balls on raised ramps while the next drum targets wait ahead.

Mapping

  • part → its own target; onset → ball arrives
  • drums → bumpers; hats → rollovers; bass → ramps
  • held note → captive ramp or spinner for its span
  • ties → counted multiball; next onsets → dotted path
  • lowest regular drum → flippers; section → lighting

Build

  • Canvas 2D · score/window · no changes
  • effort large · risk: crossing routes
  • paths, holds and counts from notes + now
  • dense targets → numbered jackpots
