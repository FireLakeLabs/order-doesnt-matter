Shows

A concert lighting rig run by the score. Each instrument owns fixtures: held notes keep their beams on, short ones flash, the bass washes the floor.

Mapping

  • most regular unpitched part → strobe · lowest pitched part → floor wash
  • every other instrument → a pair of moving heads in its colour
  • onset → flash · held note → beam stays on for its span
  • gain → brightness · pitch → beam angle, higher aims wider
  • section change → the whole rig switches look

Build

  • three.js for haze and beams · score · window
  • roles chosen from the notes, never names
  • over 8 instruments → heads share fixtures
