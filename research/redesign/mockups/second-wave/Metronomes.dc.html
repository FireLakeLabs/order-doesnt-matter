Shows

Each instrument keeps time like a metronome. Parts that hit together swing together; a polyrhythm drifts in and out of step.

Mapping

  • onset → the arm reaches an extreme, alternating sides
  • between onsets → an eased swing, worked out from now
  • held note → the swing it covers stays lit
  • hits per cycle → the weight's height on the arm
  • resting → arm parked upright, body dimmed

Build

  • Canvas 2D · score · window
  • 20 instruments → a 5 × 4 grid
  • over 32 hits a cycle → the arm blurs into a fan
