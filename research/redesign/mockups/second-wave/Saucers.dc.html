Saucers

invasion
bar 11 · 40%

Shows

A night-sky invasion. Each instrument flies a saucer: short notes are zaps, held notes are tractor beams that lift their cargo for exactly the note's length.

Mapping

  • instrument → a saucer and its pilot · altitude → pitch; unpitched by row
  • onset → the beam fires · held note → cargo rises over its real span
  • gain → beam width · resting → lights off, drifting
  • room (orbit) → a mothership; saucers sending to it fly in its light
  • section change → the mothership arrives or leaves

Build

  • Canvas 2D · score · window
  • cargo picked from a small set by row, never by name
  • over 10 instruments → saucers fly in squadrons
