Marble run

landing
bar 11 · 40% · window 2

Shows

Every note is a marble, launched one window ahead and landing exactly when it sounds. Held notes roll along their bar.

Mapping

  • onset → the marble lands on its drum, cymbal or bar
  • pitched instrument → one bar per pitch it uses, lower is longer
  • held note → the marble rolls along its bar for the real span
  • gain → marble size
  • Window → how early marbles leave the hopper

Build

  • Canvas 2D · score · window
  • drum or bars chosen from the notes, never names
  • dense parts → marbles merge into a stream
