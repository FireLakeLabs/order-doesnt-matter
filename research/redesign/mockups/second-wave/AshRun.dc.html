Ash Run

bar 11 · 40% · window 1

Shows

A lone runner crossing a dead city at full tilt. The bass builds the rooftops, the drums set the stride and the obstacles, the tempo is the speed.

Mapping

  • lowest pitched part → rooftops, one per note, its real length; pitch → height
  • bass onset → a leap to the next roof · bass rest → a chasm
  • most regular drum → footfalls · snare → a barrier smashed on its onset
  • kick → dust off the roof · open hat → sparks from dead cables
  • moving setting → the walker's searchlight, one angle per note

Build

  • Canvas 2D, four parallax layers · score · window
  • section → a district with its own ruins
  • tempo after Speed → run speed
