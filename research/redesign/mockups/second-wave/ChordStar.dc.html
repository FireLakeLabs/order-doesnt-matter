Shows

Harmony at a glance. The pitches sounding now light up on the circle of fifths and join into a shape, so chords you know become shapes you know.

Mapping

  • pitch class → its place on the circle of fifths; octave ignored
  • sounding notes → lit in their instrument's colour; lowest → heavier
  • several at once → joined into a polygon
  • chord before → fading outline · next chord → dotted, from the window
  • a clear triad is named; anything else lists its notes

Build

  • Canvas 2D · score · window
  • pitch from note, freq, or n with a scale
  • unpitched instruments listed, never dropped
