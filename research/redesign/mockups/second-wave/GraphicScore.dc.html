Graphic score

poster · Riso theme

Shows

The whole song as a graphic score you could print and hang. Time and pitch are real; each kind of sound gets its own mark.

Mapping

  • time → across, proportional · pitch → up · section → a numbered panel
  • pitched note → a line at its pitch and length; gain → weight
  • unpitched note → a mark on its own row: dot, cross or tick by length
  • moving setting → a zigzag whose width follows the value per note
  • room send → a soft halo · note carried over → dashes

Build

  • Canvas 2D · score · whole song
  • Download as PNG or SVG at poster size
  • marks drawn with multiply, so inks overprint
