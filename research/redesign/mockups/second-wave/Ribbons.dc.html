Shows

Each instrument is a silk ribbon streaming through a curtain of light at now. Ribbons open where they play and turn when a setting moves.

Mapping

  • time → right to left; the future recedes, the past trails off
  • note → the ribbon opens for its real span; gain → width
  • resting → a thread, never a gap
  • moving setting → a new turn at each onset, held for the note
  • height → pitch; unpitched instruments stack above

Build

  • three.js · score · window
  • camera drifts slowly; Window sets the reach
  • 20 instruments → ribbons fan out in depth
