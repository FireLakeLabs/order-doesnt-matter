Shows

Score meets sound. The measured output scrolls as a spectrogram with the scored notes outlined on top, so what rings on after a note ends is plain to see.

Mapping

  • needs audio → output spectrum, low at the bottom, log scale
  • brightness → measured level; heat colours mean measured only
  • scored note → outline at its pitch and real span, instrument colour
  • unpitched notes → spans on the floor strip
  • after now → dashed outlines only; nothing is measured yet

Build

  • Canvas 2D · audio + score · window
  • measured and scored always labelled apart
  • tails are seen, never inferred from settings
