Shows

The song as a transit map. Sections are stations, instruments are lines, and the board says who gets on and off at the next stop.

Mapping

  • section → a station, spaced by its length in bars
  • instrument → a line: solid where it plays, a dashed bypass where it rests
  • joining or leaving → the line enters or peels off at 45°
  • the train → the playhead; drag it to scrub
  • board → next station, how far, who leaves, who joins

Build

  • Canvas 2D · score · whole song
  • no sections → a station every four cycles
  • over 12 lines → resting ones share one grey bypass
