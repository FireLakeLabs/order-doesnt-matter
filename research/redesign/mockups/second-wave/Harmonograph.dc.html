Shows

Intervals made visible. Two sounding pitches drive a two-pendulum drawing: simple ratios close into calm figures, clashes tangle.

Mapping

  • lowest and highest sounding pitch → the two pendulum rates
  • their real ratio → the figure; a slight detune → slow drift
  • the pen winds inward over the shorter note's scored span
  • other sounding pairs → small figures beside it
  • colours → the two instruments that own the pitches

Build

  • Canvas 2D · score · window
  • rates from scored pitch, never from audio
  • under two pitches → "no interval now", in words
